CHAPTER I • EXECUTIVE THESIS

Architecting Enterprise Measurement at Hyperscale

"The multi-billion dollar monetization engine of modern social platforms depends not merely on inventory liquidity, but on the integrity of advertiser truth."

Over the past decade, digital ad measurement underwent tectonic structural disruption. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT), European Digital Markets Act (DMA) mandates, and the global demise of persistent third-party identity cookies eradicated traditional client-side last-touch attribution. What had historically been a tracking exercise transformed abruptly into a profound econometric challenge: how to infer true incrementality when signals are deliberately masked, aggregated, and differentially noisy.

At LinkedIn, Dr. Kirill Lebedev directs the 70+ person scientific, architectural, and engineering organization answering this question for a $1B+ Ads Measurement and Attribution line. By replacing obsolete heuristic attribution with Double Machine Learning (DML) orthogonalized estimators and secure multi-party compute (SMPC) clean room infrastructure, Dr. Lebedev's organization transformed signal loss into a strategic moat, unlocking over $100M+ in ARR AI infrastructure.
